Protein error strata

A check of whether the error statistics hold for individual proteins and across protein lengths, using the 154 CAMEO proteins and all 48 native probes.

The 398 ECOD proteins are left out because their final structures are currently unavailable, so pooled values here differ from the 551-protein cohort page. They also include 7stz_C unless you choose the view without it.

Signed error is the probe's distance minus the distance in OpenFold's final predicted structure. SD is the spread across pairs. Pooled statistics are pair-weighted, so large proteins count for more, while the protein-level columns below count each protein once.
