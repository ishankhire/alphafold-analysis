# September 1 publication plots

This bundle uses protocol-2.0 fivefold OOF results for four probe families and protein-equal means for R2 and TM-score. Their main uncertainty bands are paired-protein percentile bootstrap 95% intervals. The triangle figures are explicitly historical 111-protein placeholders in the manifest and source tables. Per-protein OLS measures unseen pairs within seen proteins; the other families measure unseen proteins. No AA-gated model is included. The two all-triangle main figures show normalized mean triangle violation only; triangle-inequality violation-fraction counterparts are supplemental. Spatial- and sequence-bin MAE currently use pair-pooled fivefold estimates as an explicit temporary placeholder; those figures have no uncertainty bands or individual-protein variants. Regenerate after copying the per-protein replay CSV to replace them automatically. See `figure_manifest.csv` and `source_data/` for exact provenance.
